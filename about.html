About Gabi

I love telling stories, talking to people, and making things: from ceramics and drawings to apps and platforms. I'm a product designer based in Brazil, and I fell in love with UX/UI during college, when my final project (a website about blood donation) showed me what design could actually do for people. I've been working in digital products ever since.

I am confident in my work when I'm the only designer, as long as I have the support of the PMs and developers along the way. Communication is one of my strongest traits, and I genuinely prefer bouncing ideas off others rather than designing in a vacuum. I enjoy discovery, and I'm happiest when research turns into a concrete, carefully crafted solution.

What drives me is getting better at the craft. I want to keep pushing my work in Figma, deepen how I integrate AI into my process, and become the kind of designer that teams rely on.
